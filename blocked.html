Account Blocked

Your account has been restricted and you cannot access the system at this time.

Why am I seeing this?

Your account has been blocked by your administrator. This typically occurs due to security concerns or policy violations. You will not be able to log in until this restriction is lifted.

What should I do?

• Contact your HR administrator or manager

• Request them to review your account status

• Do not attempt to bypass this restriction
