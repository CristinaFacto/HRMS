Account Deactivated

Your account has been deactivated and you currently cannot access the system.

Why am I seeing this?

Your account has been deactivated by an administrator. This may be due to inactivity, a role change, or an administrative decision. Unlike a blocked account, this may be reversible.

What should I do?

• Contact your HR administrator or manager

• Request a review or reactivation of your account

• Provide any necessary information they may require
